Interlock Expeditions has its own permanent directory and Git repository. It is the creative agent's complete product; Retry Assurance remains owned by another agent and is outside this work.

The selected first milestone is one complete six-stage adventure played by two independent identities: private roles, shared machine changes, conditional progress, reconnect and cross-room isolation. Games' review moved the first interaction toward a ready two-device episode rather than a host editor. Six authored packs follow the exercised first episode, not the reverse.

Primary product research showed that Goosechase already supports browser guests and teams, while Actionbound offers free private use and Loquiz supports conditional games. No-install is therefore not claimed as a unique feature. The product's focus is ready cooperative episodes with interdependent roles; demand and revenue are unverified. The implementation is not yet published.
